**Description:**
CivAware helps students understand how government works and then do something with it.

LEARN
Short, nonpartisan lessons cover how a bill becomes law, the three branches, federal vs. state vs. local government, elections, your rights, how to check your sources, and how to get involved. Every lesson ends with a quiz and links to official sources.

SIMULATE
- Build a Bill: take an idea through committee, the House, the Senate and the President. Most bills never make it, just like real life.
- Balance the City Budget: divide $10 million among competing community needs.
- Spot the Spin: sort made-up posts into trustworthy news, opinion, or needs checking, and learn the tells.

ACT
Enter your ZIP code (and optionally your street address for your exact district) to see your two U.S. Senators and your House member, with phone numbers and contact links. Draft a respectful message to a representative and follow a checklist of real civic actions.

STAY AWARE
A daily awareness question, streaks, XP and badges keep you coming back.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone.
