# Permission letter (template)

*Edit the bracketed parts. If your school or club requires its own form, use that too. Ask your teacher or club advisor for help and permission before testing.*

Dear parent or guardian,

My name is [Your Name], and I am a student at [School/Organization]. I built a free iPhone app called **CivAware** that teaches students how government works and how to take part in it. I am entering it in the Congressional App Challenge.

I would like to ask your child to try the app for about 15 minutes and answer a short quiz before and after, plus a few survey questions. This helps me learn whether the app actually helps students learn.

- **It is voluntary.** Your child can say no or stop at any time with no consequences.
- **It is anonymous.** I will not record names, only a participant number, grade level, and quiz scores.
- **No personal data is collected by the app.** CivAware has no accounts, ads, or tracking. If your child chooses to try the representative lookup, they can use a sample ZIP code instead of a real address.
- **Results** will be shared only as group averages (for example, "average score rose from X to Y").

If you agree, please sign below and return this form to me. Questions? Contact [teacher/advisor name and email].

Thank you!
[Your Name]
