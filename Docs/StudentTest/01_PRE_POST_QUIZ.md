# CivAware quick quiz (10 questions)

Give this **before** and **after** students use the app (same quiz both times, no help, no phones for lookup). It takes about 4 minutes. Circle one answer per question. Write the participant ID (not a name) at the top.

**Participant ID: ________   Grade: ______   Before / After (circle)**

1. Who can formally introduce a bill in Congress?
   a) Any citizen  b) Only a Member of Congress  c) Only the President  d) Only the Supreme Court

2. What is needed to override a presidential veto?
   a) A simple majority of the House  b) Two-thirds of both the House and the Senate  c) Approval of the Supreme Court  d) A vote of the state governors

3. Which branch of government interprets the laws?
   a) Legislative  b) Judicial  c) Executive  d) Administrative

4. How many electoral votes does a candidate need to win the presidency?
   a) 218  b) 270  c) 435  d) 538

5. Which of these is mainly decided by local government?
   a) Printing money  b) Declaring war  c) Park hours and funding  d) Immigration rules

6. How many U.S. Senators does each state have?
   a) 1  b) 2  c) It depends on population  d) 4

7. Which amendment set the national voting age at 18?
   a) 15th  b) 19th  c) 26th  d) 1st

8. What is "lateral reading"?
   a) Reading very fast  b) Opening other tabs to check what others say about a source or claim  c) Reading only headlines  d) Reading from left to right

9. Which is a primary source about a bill?
   a) A friend's summary  b) The bill's official text  c) A meme about it  d) A headline

10. True or false: You must be 18 or older to contact your representative.
   a) True  b) False
