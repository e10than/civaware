# Test session script (about 25 minutes per student or small group)

**Before you start:** get permission from a teacher/advisor and a signed letter (02). Install the app on a phone or use the simulator. Reset progress (Me tab, then Reset all progress) between participants.

| Min | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0-1 | Say: "You're helping me test my app, not being tested yourself. If something is confusing, that's my fault, and I want to know." |
| 1-5 | **Pre-quiz** (01). No talking or phones. |
| 5-20 | **Use the app.** Suggested tasks: (1) finish one lesson, (2) play Build a Bill, (3) find your representatives (a sample ZIP such as 94301 is fine), (4) play Spot the Spin. Say nothing unless they're totally stuck. |
| 20-24 | **Post-quiz** (same quiz). |
| 24-27 | **Survey** (03), then ask: "What did you expect to happen when you tapped X?" |

## Observation sheet (write while they use the app)
- Where did they hesitate or tap the wrong thing?
- What did they laugh at, or say "oh!" about?
- Did they read the text or skip it?
- Did they turn sound off? Did sound help or annoy?

## After the sessions
1. Enter scores in `04_RESULTS_TEMPLATE.csv`.
2. Run `python3 Docs/tools/summarize_results.py Docs/StudentTest/04_RESULTS_TEMPLATE.csv`.
3. Pick **one** thing to change based on feedback and write down: what students said, what you changed, and why. That's a great story for the application.
