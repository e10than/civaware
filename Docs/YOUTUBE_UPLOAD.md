# Uploading the demo video (2 minutes)

The Congressional App Challenge wants a **public https link**. A YouTube link is the safest choice because judges can watch it in the browser without downloading anything.

1. Go to **https://studio.youtube.com** and sign in (use an account you're comfortable having your name on).
2. Click **Create > Upload videos** and choose `Docs/CivAware_demo_screen.mp4`.
3. **Title:** `CivAware: Congressional App Challenge Demo`
4. **Description:** paste this:

   > CivAware is an iPhone app that teaches students how government works and helps them take part in it. Learn short lessons, play simulations (Build a Bill, Spot the Spin), and find your real representatives. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Code: https://github.com/e10than/civaware. Built for the Congressional App Challenge.

5. **Audience:** choose **No, it's not made for kids** (YouTube requires you to choose; the app is for teens).
6. **Visibility:** choose **Unlisted** (anyone with the link can watch, and it won't show in searches) or **Public**. Do NOT choose Private, because judges couldn't open it.
7. Click **Save**, then copy the video link (it looks like `https://youtu.be/XXXXXXXXXXX`).
8. Open the link in a private/incognito window to confirm it plays without signing in.
9. Paste the link into the video field of your application and click **Save Draft**.

If you record your own voiceover first (iMovie), upload that version instead.
