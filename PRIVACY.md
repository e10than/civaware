# CivAware Privacy Policy

*Effective October 1, 2026*

CivAware is a free iPhone app that teaches students how government works and helps them take part in it. It is built to collect as little as possible.

## What CivAware does not do
- No accounts or sign-in.
- No advertising and no ad networks.
- No analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
- No collection of names, emails, phone numbers, or contacts.
- It does not sell or share your data.

## What stays on your device
Your progress (lessons completed, quiz scores, XP, streaks, badges, and the last ZIP code you entered) is stored only on your device. You can erase it any time from **Me > Reset all progress**, or by deleting the app.

## Network use (the "Find my representatives" feature)
When you use this feature, CivAware contacts these public services:
- **zippopotam.us** receives the ZIP code you enter, to look up your state.
- **U.S. Census Geocoder** (geocoding.geo.census.gov) receives your ZIP code and street address, only if you choose to enter an address, to find your congressional district.
- **unitedstates.github.io** (the open-source *congress-legislators* dataset and member photos) is downloaded to show current Members of Congress and their official contact information.

CivAware does not send this information to any server run by the developer, and it does not save your street address. Each service has its own privacy practices. Links in the app (for example to vote.gov or official sources) open in your browser.

## Children
CivAware does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is designed for students and contains no ads, chat, or user-generated content.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
